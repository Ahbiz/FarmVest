FarmVest is a licensed agricultural fintech platform that pools investor capital to fund high-yield commercial farming projects. Professional agronomists manage operations, and upon harvest sale, capital and profits are distributed directly into your secure wallet.
Historical yields range between 14.8% and 22.4% APY depending on crop category and cycle length (typically 6 to 18 months). Yield payouts are automatically deposited into your FarmVest wallet upon harvest sale completion.
100% of capital principal in FarmVest yield pools is insured by Grade-A multi-peril crop insurance policies covering drought, flood, pest outbreaks, and extreme weather. In the event of crop damage, insurance payouts reimburse the pool principal.
You can start co-funding with as little as $90 per share (e.g. Hydroponic Blueberry Pool). Minimum share amounts vary per crop cycle to ensure accessible entry for all investors.
Before any pool opens for funding, FarmVest executes legally binding wholesale off-take contracts with major grain distributors, food processors, and supermarket chains, locking in guaranteed purchase prices before planting begins.
Yes. Every co-funder receives full dashboard access to live IoT telemetry data, satellite soil moisture indexing, drone orthomosaic imagery, and bi-weekly agronomist video logs for their active pools.
All investor capital is held in SEC-regulated, bank-grade escrow accounts. Funds are disbursed to farm managers in milestone-based tranches verified by third-party agricultural auditors.
