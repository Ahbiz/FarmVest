What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They allow the site to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and understand how you interact with its pages. We also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, and software development kits, which we refer to collectively as "cookies" throughout this policy.

Cookies may be "session" cookies, which expire when you close your browser, or "persistent" cookies, which remain until they expire or you delete them. Some are set by FarmVest directly ("first-party") and others by our service providers ("third-party").

Types of Cookies We Use

We group the cookies used on the FarmVest platform into four categories. Essential cookies are always active because the platform cannot function securely without them; the remaining categories are optional and controlled by your consent preferences.

Category Purpose Consent
Essential Enable core functions such as secure login, session management, fraud prevention, and load balancing. Always active
Analytics Help us understand how investors navigate the platform so we can improve performance and usability. Optional
Functional Remember preferences such as language, currency, and dashboard layout to personalize your experience. Optional
Marketing Measure the effectiveness of our campaigns and show you relevant information about new yield pools. Optional

Managing Cookies

When you first visit FarmVest, our cookie banner lets you accept or reject optional cookie categories. You can revisit and change these choices at any time through the cookie-preferences link in the footer of our platform.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings, including deleting existing cookies and blocking new ones. Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent parts of the platform from working correctly, including secure login and investment functions.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by trusted third parties who provide services on our behalf, such as analytics providers, fraud-detection tools, and advertising partners. These providers may use cookies to collect information about your activity across different websites.

We do not control third-party cookies. We encourage you to review the privacy and cookie policies of these providers for details of their practices. Information we collect through cookies is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use or in applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the platform.
