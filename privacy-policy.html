Introduction

FarmVest Inc. ("FarmVest," "we," "us," or "our") operates an agriculture investment platform that connects investors with sustainable farming yield pools. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect the personal information you provide when you create an account, complete identity verification, fund a pool, or otherwise interact with our website and services.

By accessing or using the FarmVest platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Because we operate as a regulated financial services provider, some of our data practices are mandated by anti-money-laundering, securities, and consumer-protection laws, and cannot be waived at your request.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, information generated automatically as you use the platform, and information received from trusted third parties such as identity-verification and payment providers.

  • Account and profile data: your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, and login credentials.
  • Financial and investment data: bank account and payment details, transaction history, pool allocations, payout records, and tax identifiers.
  • Verification data: government-issued identification documents, proof of address, and biometric selfie checks submitted during onboarding.
  • Technical data: IP address, device identifiers, browser type, and activity logs collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the platform, process investments and payouts, and meet our legal obligations. Specifically, we use it to open and administer your account, allocate funds to the yield pools you select, calculate and distribute returns, and communicate service updates.

We also process data to detect and prevent fraud, comply with anti-money-laundering and sanctions screening requirements, personalize your dashboard experience, and, where you have consented, send you information about new pools and promotions. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

KYC & Identity Verification

As a financial platform, FarmVest is legally required to perform Know Your Customer (KYC) and Customer Due Diligence checks before you can invest or withdraw funds. During onboarding we collect and verify identity documents, and we may re-verify your identity periodically or when unusual activity is detected.

Verification is performed together with specialist identity-verification partners who process your documents on our behalf under strict contractual controls. We retain verification records for the minimum period required by applicable financial regulations, even after your account is closed.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share personal information only where necessary to deliver our services or comply with the law. This includes payment processors and banking partners, identity-verification and fraud-prevention providers, cloud hosting and analytics vendors, and professional advisors such as auditors and legal counsel.

We may also disclose information to regulators, law-enforcement agencies, or courts when required by a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of FarmVest, our investors, or the public. All service providers are bound by contractual obligations to safeguard your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

Data Security

We employ industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit and at rest, network segmentation, role-based access controls, and continuous monitoring. Sensitive verification documents are stored in restricted environments accessible only to authorized personnel.

While we work hard to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to enable two-factor authentication on your account.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to request a portable copy of the data you provided to us. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Please note that some rights are limited where we are legally required to retain data — for example, KYC and transaction records that we must keep to satisfy financial-compliance obligations. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings and our on-site preference tools. For a full explanation of the categories of cookies we use and how to manage them, please review our Cookie Policy.

International Transfers

FarmVest serves investors globally, and your information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you reside, including the United States. These countries may have data-protection laws that differ from those in your home jurisdiction.

When we transfer personal data across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and equivalent mechanisms to ensure your information continues to receive an adequate level of protection.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any of your privacy rights, please contact our Data Protection team at support@farmvest.com. You may also write to us at FarmVest Inc., 100 Innovation Park, AgriTech Way, San Francisco, CA 94105, United States.
