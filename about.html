Empowering Agri-Fintech

Bridging Capital Markets & Sustainable Yields

Founded in 2024 by experienced agronomists and financial analysts, FarmVest was created to fix the structural gap in farm co-funding. We provide retail and institutional co-funders direct access to high-yield commercial crop and livestock cycles.

Every project on our platform undergoes rigorous 3-stage agronomist due diligence, soil telemetry analysis, and binding off-take buyer contracting before being listed for co-funding.
