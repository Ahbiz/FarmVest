Overview

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which funds may be returned to investors on the FarmVest platform, how and when payouts are processed, and the fees that apply when investors exit a yield pool before its scheduled maturity date. Please read this policy carefully before committing funds to any pool.

FarmVest operates as an agricultural investment platform that deploys investor capital into real farming operations. Because capital is put to productive use and exposed to market and operational risk, the refund landscape differs from that of a standard retail product or subscription service. All investment decisions should be made with a full understanding of the terms set out below and in our Terms of Service.

Investment Nature & No Guaranteed Refunds

All funds committed to a FarmVest yield pool constitute an investment in an agricultural enterprise. Returns are not guaranteed; your capital is at risk. FarmVest does not offer an unconditional refund right on invested funds the way a merchant might offer a product return, because the capital is deployed to farming operations that cannot be instantly reversed.

Projected annual returns displayed on pool pages are based on agronomic modeling and historical performance data. Actual returns may be higher or lower, and in adverse circumstances — severe weather events, pest outbreaks, commodity-price collapses, or force-majeure events — a pool may return less than the principal invested. In such cases FarmVest will communicate transparently with investors and follow the dispute process described below.

Cooling-Off Period

Investors who have made an initial commitment to a yield pool benefit from a 48-hour cooling-off period starting from the moment the investment is confirmed in their dashboard. During this window you may cancel your commitment and receive a full refund of the committed amount, provided the pool has not yet reached its funding close date and your funds have not been disbursed to the farm operator.

To exercise your cooling-off right, submit a cancellation request through your dashboard or contact our support team at support@farmvest.com within 48 hours of the confirmation timestamp. Refunds processed under the cooling-off right are returned to your FarmVest wallet within 1 business day and to your linked bank account within 3–5 business days thereafter.

Withdrawal of Uninvested Funds

Funds deposited to your FarmVest wallet that have not yet been committed to any yield pool — for example, funds awaiting a pool opening or funds returned from a matured pool — are withdrawable at any time with no fee or penalty.

Withdrawal requests for uninvested wallet balances are processed within 1 business day of submission, subject to identity-verification status and compliance checks. Funds arrive in your linked bank account within 3–5 business days depending on your financial institution. FarmVest does not charge processing fees on uninvested-fund withdrawals; however, your bank may apply standard international transfer fees for cross-border payments.

Pool Maturity & Payouts

Each yield pool has a defined maturity date disclosed on the pool detail page before you invest. On or shortly after this date, FarmVest settles the pool: the principal and any accrued returns are calculated, platform-management fees and performance fees are deducted according to the fee schedule disclosed at investment time, and the net amount is credited to investors' FarmVest wallets.

Standard payout processing takes up to 10 business days following the pool maturity date to allow for harvest settlement, commodity sale confirmation, and fund reconciliation. Investors are notified by email when their wallet has been credited. From there, funds may be reinvested in a new pool or withdrawn to a bank account at no additional charge.

Early Exit Fees

If you wish to exit a yield pool before its maturity date, FarmVest may facilitate an early exit subject to availability of a buyer on our secondary marketplace. Early exits are not guaranteed — they depend on another investor being willing to purchase your position at an agreed price.

Where an early exit is completed, an early exit fee of 2% of the exited value applies to cover marketplace facilitation costs. This fee is deducted before the remaining proceeds are credited to your wallet. Additionally, any accrued returns credited up to the exit date may be subject to a partial-period performance fee as specified on the pool's fee schedule. FarmVest reserves the right to suspend the secondary marketplace during periods of high volatility or operational disruption.

Dispute Resolution

If you believe a payout, fee deduction, or refund has been processed incorrectly, you must notify FarmVest in writing within 30 days of the transaction date. Submit your dispute via email to support@farmvest.com with your account ID, the transaction reference, and a clear description of the discrepancy. We will acknowledge your dispute within 2 business days and aim to resolve it within 14 business days.

If a dispute cannot be resolved through our internal process, it may be escalated to mediation under the rules of a mutually agreed arbitration body. Nothing in this policy prevents you from pursuing remedies available to you under consumer-protection or investor-rights legislation in your jurisdiction.

Contact Us

For questions about this Refund Policy, refund requests, payout timelines, or disputes, please reach our team at support@farmvest.com or write to FarmVest Inc., 100 Innovation Park, AgriTech Way, San Francisco, CA 94105, United States.
